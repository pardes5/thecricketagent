ABOUT THE AGENT

Waheed Shirzay — ECB Registered Cricket Agent.

Waheed Shirzay is a UK-based, ECB Registered Cricket Agent specialising in player representation. Operating in line with ECB Agent Regulations and PCA standards, he offers a compliant, transparent and personal alternative to larger agencies. For cricketers — from aspiring professionals to overseas talent looking to play in the UK — Waheed sources and secures contracts at county, league, club and overseas level, negotiates fair terms, and provides honest career guidance. He also supports overseas players through UK visa and ECB endorsement requirements. Clubs looking to recruit can browse Waheed's full register of available, vetted players on his website. Every player is known individually — communication is direct, and no deal is pursued unless it genuinely serves the player's best interests.
