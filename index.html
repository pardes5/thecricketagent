WHO WE ARE

At The Cricket Scouts, we are driven by a simple yet powerful mission: to help talented cricketers navigate their journey in the game with clarity, integrity, and confidence.

Our team operates with unwavering integrity at every step. We work strictly in accordance with the ECB Players' Agent Registration Regulations and uphold the highest ethical standards — because trust is the foundation of everything we do. We believe that transparency, honesty, and full compliance with cricket's governing rules are not just requirements — they are our promise to every player we represent.

We are genuinely passionate about what we do. Cricket runs through our veins, and we take immense pride in seeing players realise their potential and achieve their dreams on and off the field. Every opportunity we secure, every contract we review, and every career decision we help make is guided by one core principle: your success is our success.
